About the Event

Welcome to Tuliptopia🌷, a paradise of petals, colors, and wonders open all spring for anyone looking to enjoy the outdoors. It's the perfect weekend escape to hang out with loved ones, take aesthetic photos, or wander and build your own custom bouquets.

Just grab a basket at the gate, and pick your favorite blooms. We can't wait to see you out in paradise.

What to Expect

A magical spring experience for all ages! ✨

  • 20+ Tulip Varieties: Explore a breathtaking sea of rare fringes, classic pastels, and vibrant spring colors.
  • Instagram-Ready Backdrops: Snap the perfect photo at our beautifully designed floral swings and rustic windmills.
  • Live music concerts: Unwind and groove to local acoustic artists performing live in the picnic groves every weekend.
